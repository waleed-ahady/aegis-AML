- IBM AML-Data repository: https://github.com/IBM/AML-Data
- IBM Transactions for Anti Money Laundering dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ealtman2019/ibm-transactions-for-anti-money-laundering-aml
- IBM Research publication page: https://research.ibm.com/publications/realistic-synthetic-financial-transactions-for-anti-money-laundering-models
- NeurIPS 2023 paper: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/5f38404edff6f3f642d6fa5892479c42-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf

The original paper explains the motivation for synthetic financial data, the virtual-world generator, complete ground-truth labels, dataset variants, and graph-learning benchmarks. Cite the paper and dataset according to the publisher's instructions when publishing research based on the data.
